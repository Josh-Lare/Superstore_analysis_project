# Superstore Sales Analysis

An exploratory data analysis (EDA) project using a real-world retail dataset to uncover business insights across regions, categories, customer segments, and discount behaviour.

## Business Questions Answered
1. Which region performs best in sales, profit, and profit margin?
2. Which product categories and sub-categories are most profitable?
3. What does the monthly sales trend look like across the year?
4. Which customer segment generates the most revenue?
5. What is the relationship between discounting and profit?

## Key Findings
- West region leads in both sales ($725K) and profit margin (14.94%)
- Central region over-discounts — lowest profit margin at 7.92%
- Furniture is barely profitable at only 2% margin
- Technology copiers are the most profitable sub-category
- Consumer segment accounts for over 50% of total revenue
- Higher discounts consistently reduce profit without boosting sales
